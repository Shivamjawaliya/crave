How to Prevent Render Free Tier from Sleeping (Cold Starts)

Render's free tier spins down (sleeps) after 15 minutes of inactivity, which can cause a 30-second delay on the first user request. You can prevent this completely for free!

1. Go to cron-job.org or uptimerobot.com (100% Free).

2. Create a HTTP monitor pointing to: https://food-platform-api.onrender.com/api/v1/health

3. Set the interval to ping every 10 minutes.

✨ This keeps your Render server warm, active, and instant 24 hours a day with 0 cold starts!
